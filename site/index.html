On a real Laptop 16

What it does

Both modules, side by side

A left and a right canvas, laid out the way the modules sit next to your keyboard. Modules are found automatically. No COM ports to pick, no browser permissions.

Draw in greyscale

Left-click to draw, right-click to erase, drag to paint. Set the brush brightness to anything from a faint glow to full white.

Animations that loop perfectly

Digital rain, fire, plasma, a DNA helix, a heartbeat and more. Each one returns exactly to its first frame, so there's never a jump. Simple downward scrolls run on the module itself with no work for your PC.

Text that fits, plus CRT effects

Type a word and it's fitted to the module, stacked, sideways or as a ticker, still or sliding. Add a retro scanline look, or show the time and battery with {time} and {battery}.

Patterns in one click

Checkerboards, diagonal lines, gradients, a diamond, zig-zag, scatter, and progress-bar percentages. Every pattern you pick can still be edited by hand.

Lives in the tray

Starts with Windows, stays out of the way, and uses almost no memory while the window is closed. The tray menu lets you keep the LEDs on or off whenever you want.

Remembers everything

Your drawings, brightness and module names come back after a restart, a sleep, or after you swap a module out.

Feels like Windows

Follows your light or dark mode and accent color, scales cleanly on any display, and works with the keyboard and screen readers.

It turns itself off when you're not looking

The app watches whether the laptop's own screen is in use. If it isn't, the LEDs fade out. Each rule can be switched off in the app.

Install

  1. Download the installer from the latest release.
  2. Run it. No admin rights are needed. It installs just for you.
    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Leave "Start automatically when I sign in" ticked if you want the LEDs managed from the moment you log in.
  4. Find the LED icon in the system tray. Click it to open the editor, right-click it for quick options.

Questions

It says a module is "in use by another app"

Only one program can talk to a module at a time. Close the ledmatrix.frame.work web page, Framework's inputmodule-control tool, or any other LED app, and the manager connects on its own within a few seconds.

My drawings show up on the wrong side

Press "Swap left / right" in the app. Use "Identify" to blink a module if you're not sure which one is which.

Why do the LEDs go dark on their own with other tools?

The module's firmware turns the LEDs off after 60 seconds without hearing from the computer. The manager sends a small signal every 30 seconds to keep them on, and stays silent when they're meant to be off.

Does it need the internet or collect any data?

No. It never connects to the network and sends nothing anywhere. See Privacy.

Can I uninstall it cleanly?

Yes, from Settings > Apps. The uninstaller removes the startup entry and asks whether to keep your saved drawings and settings.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends nothing. It never connects to the internet, and everything it saves stays on your PC.

What the app saves, on your PC only

  • Your settings and drawings, in %AppData%\DeoLedMatrixManager.
  • A log of warnings and errors, in %LocalAppData%\DeoLedMatrixManager\logs. With Debug mode on, it also logs what the app does and writes a once-a-minute performance file; those files are deleted after 14 days. Logs describe the app and the modules (ports, USB slots, firmware version, CPU and memory use), not you.
  • The Microsoft Store copy keeps these files in its own private folder, which Windows deletes when you uninstall it. The GitHub installer's uninstaller asks whether to delete them.

What the app reads

  • Your PC's manufacturer and model, to check it's a Framework Laptop 16.
  • Lid, screen and power state, to switch the LEDs off and on.
  • Time, date, battery level, and CPU and RAM use, only when you put them in your text.

These are used on your PC as the app runs and are never sent anywhere. There's no telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no account and no update check. Links in the app open in your web browser.

This website

The site has no cookies and no analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google see your IP address when they send you the page and the font.
